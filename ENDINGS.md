# Endings record

Criteria for a recorded ending (from the mission brief):
1. reached through legitimate gameplay,
2. its conclusion actually witnessed,
3. evidence preserved (frames + journal entry),
4. the observable difference from other endings explained.

Nothing is known about how many endings exist or what causes them. The title
screen has no ending counter. Records are appended only when an ending is
witnessed.
